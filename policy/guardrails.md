# Guardrails

## Layers

1. Input policy
2. Retrieval policy
3. Tool policy
4. Generation policy
5. Output policy
6. Approval policy

## Required outcomes

1. Unsafe or out-of-scope requests are refused or escalated.
2. Missing evidence is disclosed.
3. Clinical determinations are not made by the agent.
4. Human review is required for transition-exception packets and follow-up actions.
